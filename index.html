Our Story

A little tide pool
in the middle of the city

Moānā means ocean — and that's exactly the feeling we chase behind every counter. Woven pendant lights, driftwood arches and hand-painted surfboards set the scene; what fills your cup is the rest of the story. We source real fruit, real espresso, and real patience: fresh mint muddled to order, milk steamed to a whisper, matcha whisked the slow way.

No two afternoons here look the same — sunlight through the monstera leaves at noon, string lights and quiet conversation once the heat breaks. Pull up a rattan chair. You're on island time now.
